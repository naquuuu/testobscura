obscur4 home hero: three directions

Design decision log / 2026-10-08 / status: decided

Decision

Winner: A - Artifact-led.

Why A

  • Shows the actual product (a localized lure) instead of describing it, so the page anchors to the real thing rather than a generic look.
  • Teaches the viewer something in the first screen: how to spot the signs.
  • Motion carries meaning: the cues appear in order.

Why not B

Brand-forward but says nothing about the service; reads like a template.

Why not C

Accurate but explains the platform before the buyer's problem; better placed on How We Work (reused there).

Review question

Asked of every direction: who is this for, what are we communicating, does it need a name or can it stay invisible?

A

ForSecurity, risk and audit leads at Indonesian organisations who need awareness evidence.

CommunicatingWe run the real thing in your language, and you can learn the signs from it.

NameCan stay invisible: the lure carries the page, the name lives in the nav.

B

ForAnyone; no specific buyer is addressed above the headline.

CommunicatingThat obscur4 exists. Nothing about phishing or assessments until the text below.

NameNeeds the name: it is the only thing on screen.

C

ForTechnical evaluators checking how the platform is controlled.

CommunicatingWork is authorized and scoped before anything runs, and ends in evidence.

NameCan stay invisible: the diagram carries it; no wordmark needed.

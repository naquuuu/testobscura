AboutOne platform, three services

Security services for Indonesian organizations, run on one platform.

obscur4 provides security awareness, secure development and mobile app assessment, delivered through one platform so that every engagement produces the same kind of evidence.

How we operate

  1. Intake, scope and authorization are recorded in the platform.
  2. Execution runs from it.
  3. Reports and evidence are generated by it; human work is limited to final sign-off.
  4. Each engagement adds a reusable template or runbook.

Why Bahasa Indonesia matters

Bahasa Indonesia is the language of the people we test. Every lure and training module is written in it, for Indonesian workplaces.

Published material

We publish case material only with the client's written permission and in sanitized form.

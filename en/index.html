How a campaign works

From one message to evidence your auditors can follow

  1. It starts with one message.

    Lures are written from message types common in Indonesian workplaces: parcel deliveries, bank notices, tax, a note from a manager.

  2. The signs are marked.

    Every lure carries the signs staff should learn to notice: a sender that does not match its domain, time pressure, a link to somewhere else.

  3. The campaign reaches every target group.

    Campaigns are scoped and authorized first, then launched from our platform to the groups you choose.

  4. Results come back by group.

    Reports show how each group responded, and training follows where it is needed. Person-level results stay with administrators you designate.

  5. Every step leaves evidence.

    Each campaign ends in a platform-generated report your audit and risk teams can follow.

Authorization, evidence and data handling

Financial regulators set cyber-resilience testing expectations, and the personal data protection law (UU PDP) applies to any organization that processes personal data. Your team needs testing it can account for.

Authorization

No work starts without a written authorization record that sets the scope. We do not work outside that scope.

Evidence

Every reported finding traces to a stored artifact and a recorded command sequence.

Sanitization and data

Test environments are disposable and segmented. Each client's data is kept apart, exports are redacted, and identifiers are removed from anything shared with a vendor.
